# Weekly research log (Sundays): competitors + trends
Format: date · finding · how we used it

## 2026-11-22
Sources: Meta Ad Library (Israel, active ads, "בניית אתרים" ~270 results, "דף נחיתה"), sorted by impressions; WebSearch on Facebook Reels practices 2026.
- Most Israeli web-design ads open with "בעל עסק?" and promise results ("אתר מקצועי שיכפיל את המכירות שלך", "שיווק דיגיטלי עם תוצאות"), end in a lead form. (ShaharDigital running since 06/2026, Fullpower since 11/2025.) → We stand out by NOT promising results and by showing the site before payment.
- Monthly pricing is common: "בניית אתר החל מ־495 ש״ח בחודש ... ללא התחייבות" (Wix-based studio, running since 05/2026). → Our fixed one-time price is a real contrast, but before using "no monthly fee" confirm with Yoad what hosting/domain cost.
- One studio (סטודיו פרץ, since 07/2026) opens with a client quote as the hook ("אין לי אתר. אין לי מכירות..."), then the story. Quote hooks run long = probably work. → Used today: reel "״אין לי זמן לאתר.״" (objection as quote, not a fake testimonial).
- Nobody in the top results targets lawyers specifically, and nobody offers a free sketch before payment. → Keep owning "עורכי דין" + "רואים לפני שמשלמים".
- Reels guidance (2026 guides: hook in first 1–3s, text on screen for sound-off, 7–15s, loop) matches our rules; nothing new to change.
- Idea of the week: objection-quote reel series ("אין לי זמן", "יש לי אינסטגרם", "זה יקר") – one per week.
